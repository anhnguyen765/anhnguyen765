# Hey, I'm Bo 👋

*(Anh on paper, Bo to everyone else. Put "Product Owner" after it and you get the **Broduct Owner**. Yes, that stuck.)*

I work on payments at **Zalopay**, one of Vietnam's largest e-wallets. For a while I looked after the unglamorous plumbing: the ledgers, refunds and acquiring systems that quietly move millions of transactions a day. I also worked on the cross-border rails (Alipay+, Weixin Pay) that let Vietnamese travellers pay abroad without hunting for an ATM.

These days I've started exploring **product strategy**. Mostly that means I chase the questions nobody's roadmap covers:

> Why do people finish eKYC and then never pay?
> What actually gets someone to try BNPL?
> What makes them come back?

If you've got a theory, I've probably already written a SQL query to try to disprove it. 🕵️

---

### 🧭 How I like to work

I go where the users are. I once stood at the Vietnam–China border just to watch real QR payments succeed and fail. Dashboards are great, but they don't tell you the shop's Wi-Fi is down.

I test my hunches. Cohort data, SQL, PySpark. And when my favourite hypothesis turns out to be wrong, I'll say so out loud.

And if the tool I need doesn't exist, I build it. I studied CS, used to do full-stack dev, and these days I write my own little AI helpers to speed up product work.

### 🌱 The side quest

Outside Zalopay, I'm building tech talent programs at **[Project X Vietnam](https://www.projectxvietnam.org/)**, a free programme that connects Vietnam's top young tech talent with companies like Grab and Shopee.

I joined as a fellow in 2024 and somehow ended up running Growth. Then I tried to escape into Training for the summer... and still kept Growth. 😅 Along the way I've learned that people want useful content, not inspirational quotes (our open rates literally halved when we tried the inspirational stuff). I also still sneak commits into the website now and then, because **once an engineer, always an engineer**.
